About us

Regional expertise,
national coverage

BV IMM Brokers is an insurance brokerage firm within Millenium Insurance Broker (MIB), one of Romania's leading insurance brokers. Through this partnership, our clients benefit from the negotiating power and expertise of a group with nationwide presence.

We specialize in solutions for small and medium enterprises, as well as large-scale industrial projects. We work with over 15 licensed insurance companies in Romania, ensuring access to the most competitive market conditions, regardless of your geographic location.

Services

What we cover



SME & Corporate Insurance

Comprehensive solutions for SMEs and corporate clients: property, liability, business interruption, inventory. Proven experience in structured programmes for energy, construction and industrial sectors.

Contractors All Risks (CAR)

CAR/EAR policies for construction and assembly projects, including third-party liability on site.

Professional Indemnity

Coverage for professional errors and omissions, essential for consultants, designers, specialized contractors.

Pollution Liability

Sudden and accidental pollution coverage, mandatory in petrochemical and industrial sectors.

Fleet Insurance

RCA, Casco, Roadside Assistance and Green Card — managed as a unified programme with volume-negotiated preferential rates.

Health & Accident Insurance

Workplace accidents, group health coverage, life insurance and business travel — valued benefits for your workforce.

Consulting & Audit

Independent analysis of existing insurance portfolios, coverage gap identification, and cost optimization.

Directors & Officers

Personal liability protection for executive management and boards of directors against claims from shareholders, employees, or third parties.

Cyber Insurance

Coverage for cyber risks: ransomware attacks, data breaches, IT business interruption, and liability to third parties affected by digital security incidents.

Commercial Credit Insurance

Protection against non-payment of commercial receivables. Essential for exporters and B2B companies selling on credit terms.
